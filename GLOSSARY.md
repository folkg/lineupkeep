# Lineupkeep glossary

The [agreed domain glossary](docs/fantasy-manager/GLOSSARY.md) is authoritative. Read it before introducing product types, module names, or vocabulary.

The starter's Notes, Active Note, Trashed Note, and writing-workspace terminology describe its retained development demo only. They are not Lineupkeep product concepts and do not dictate the fantasy domain.

Keep application account, provider account, provider connection, owned fantasy team, and provider fantasy day distinct. No migration or inheritance from AutoCoach is implied.

# Notes

People keep private writing in a working set and retain discarded writing in Trash until they choose permanent deletion.

## Language

**User**: A person who signs in and owns Notes. Another User cannot see or change their writing.
_Avoid_: Account as a synonym for the person.

**Note**: A titled piece of writing with a body, owned by one User.

**Active Note**: A Note in its owner's working set, available to edit or move to Trash.

**Trashed Note**: A retained Note outside the working set, available to restore or permanently delete.
_Avoid_: Archived Note, deleted Note.

**Restore**: Return a Trashed Note to the working set.

**Permanent deletion**: Irreversible removal of a Trashed Note.

**Session**: An authenticated period identifying one User, not a synonym for a token.

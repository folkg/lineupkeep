# Lineupkeep

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Fantasy-sports managers who want their teams looked after without repeatedly checking every lineup. Initially one person with five Yahoo NFL/NHL teams; open registration is part of the planned launch. Agents use the same owned resources through stateless MCP.

## Product Purpose

Keep legal, actionable lineups ready, make automation's state understandable, and let people inspect the evidence behind a decision.

## Positioning

Your lineup. Looked after.

An independent, provider-neutral fantasy-management product. Yahoo NFL/NHL are the first integrations, not the product identity. The name joins lineup and upkeep through their shared “up.”

## Operating Context

Brief desktop and mobile checks around games, sometimes in bright daylight and sometimes in the evening. People need clear enrollment, connection health, run outcomes, and recovery—not another analytics dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Implemented Phase 1: passwordless email-code sign-in, hosted browser linking with a local-helper fallback, owner-scoped encrypted Yahoo connections, team discovery, connection and team controls, bounded provider queries, native `set_lineup`, hourly current-period automation, reconnect email, and owner-scoped feedback from the app and MCP clients.

Deferred to Phase 2: a ground-up pure lineup optimizer, legal same-roster reserve movement, and direct cutover after acceptance. No transactions, server-side LLM, premium entitlement bypass, or user scoring controls.

These describe implemented behavior and deferred scope. Do not claim an accuracy guarantee, projection, transaction execution, custom scoring, or a provider that is not connected.

## Evidence on Hand

The copied planning workspace records owner decisions, earlier Yahoo research, starter inspection, and outstanding verification gates. No customer testimonials, commercial traction, accuracy claims, or guaranteed free operation have been established. Naming screening is preliminary; no domain ownership or trademark registration is claimed.

## Product Principles

Fresh implementation, not migration or an AutoCoach port. Reconsider architecture, algorithms, infrastructure, IaC, and tests deliberately. Reuse verified knowledge, not old implementation. Prefer small provider-neutral interfaces, meaningful workflow machines, pure policy/selection logic, independently verified legality, and recoverable outcomes. “Sent” is not “verified.”

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, AA contrast, touch-sized targets, reduced motion, explicit non-colour state labels, locale-aware display, and preserved missing-versus-zero meaning.

## Brand Commitments

Lineupkeep in prose; lowercase lineupkeep in the wordmark. One continuous word, one baseline, one colour; the shared “up” is a quiet discovery. Never LineUPkeep or LineUpKeep.

Professional, solid, and energetically sporting—not cheap, mascot-led, gamified, or AI-themed. The owner selected royal violet and gold: vivid violet actions, golden yellow accents, and deep violet ink, with complete light/dark palettes. Do not substitute green or return to extreme restraint. Precise typography and useful information keep the colour confident rather than noisy. No trophies, shields, robot heads, gradient lettering, neon, or sports-betting hype. “Your lineup. Looked after.” is the positioning line, not an accuracy guarantee.

Replace the starter's theatrical prompt-book identity completely. The owner approved this direction; do not reopen a style tournament during routine implementation. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) owns tokens and application rules; [brand notes](docs/brand/BRAND.md) owns name, voice, and wordmark usage.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People capturing and revisiting private short writing.

## Product Purpose

Keep titled writing easy to capture, find, revise, and recover from Trash.

## Positioning

A focused personal writing space with explicit ownership and recoverable everyday actions.

## Operating Context

Connected browser use on desktop and mobile; keyboard and pointer interaction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Users create, search, edit, trash, and restore their own Notes; permanent deletion is irreversible.
Scoped MCP clients can read and manage Notes but cannot permanently delete them.
Server confirmation establishes saved work. Offline synchronization and draft persistence across reloads are not promised.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository implementations and tests establish current behavior. No external customer research or commercial evidence is available.

## Product Principles

Make useful actions obvious, preserve failed work, distinguish uncertain outcomes from rejection, and protect newer edits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic controls, keyboard access, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

Sourdough Starter uses a prompt-book identity: warm paper, ink, editorial hierarchy, and restrained theatrical cues.
Preserve established copy and visual intent during unrelated narrow changes; [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) owns visual guidance.
